Proposition 2.3.14.

Suppose we have vectors \(\vec{v_1},\vec{v_2},\ldots,\vec{v_n}\) in \(\real^m\text{.}\) Then \(\laspan{\vec{v_1},\vec{v_2},\ldots,\vec{v_n}}=\real^m\) if and only if the matrix \(\left[\begin{array}{rrrr} \vec{v_1}\amp\vec{v_2}\amp\cdots\amp\vec{v_n} \end{array}\right]\) has a pivot position in every row.
