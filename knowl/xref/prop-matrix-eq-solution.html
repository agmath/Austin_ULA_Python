Proposition 2.2.4.

If \(A=\left[\begin{array}{rrrr} \vec{v_1}\amp\vec{v_2}\amp\ldots\vec{v_n} \end{array}\right]\) and \(\vec{x}=\left[ \begin{array}{c} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \\ \end{array}\right] \text{,}\) then the following statements are equivalent.
  • The vector \(\vec{x}\) satisfies the equation \(A\vec{x} = \vec{b} \text{.}\)
  • The vector \(\vec{b}\) is a linear combination of the columns of \(A\) with weights \(x_j\text{:}\)
    \begin{equation*} x_1\vec{v_1} + x_2\vec{v_2} + \ldots + x_n\vec{v_n} = \vec{b}\text{.} \end{equation*}
  • The components of \(\vec{x}\) form a solution to the linear system corresponding to the augmented matrix
    \begin{equation*} \left[\begin{array}{rrrr|r} \vec{v_1} \amp \vec{v_2} \amp \cdots \amp \vec{v_n} \amp \vec{b} \end{array}\right]\text{.} \end{equation*}
